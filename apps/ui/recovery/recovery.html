Connecting to your workspace

Checking the local service and desktop providers.

Find the connected window

  1. Open Mission Control and look for another Thesaurus window. You can also check each Thesaurus icon in the Dock.
  2. Continue your work there. To use this copy instead, save any edits and quit the other copy from its Thesaurus menu.
  3. Return here and retry the connection. Closing a window alone may leave that copy running.
Can’t find another window?

This screen cannot identify which copy holds the connection, or whether that copy has just stopped. Check for an extra Thesaurus app in Activity Monitor. If you recently quit it, wait a moment and retry.

The service and its work can continue independently of this window.
